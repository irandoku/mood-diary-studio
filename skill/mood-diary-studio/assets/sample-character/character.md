## Canonical identity

- A small, simple paper-shaped mascot.
- Quiet, readable silhouette with one folded upper-right corner.
- Two oval eyes, no visible mouth, and a muted-blue cross-body satchel.

## Visual anchors

### Silhouette and proportions

- Rounded rectangular off-white paper body, taller than wide.
- One clearly folded upper-right corner.
- Short limbs and a centered upright stance.

### Head, face, and expression baseline

- Face appears directly on the upper half of the paper body.
- Two simple charcoal oval eyes.
- No visible mouth, eyebrows, nose, or separate head.

### Body, limbs, and appendages

- Two short charcoal arms with round ends.
- Two short charcoal legs with simple oval feet.
- No ears, horns, hair, wings, or tail.

### Colors and markings

- Warm off-white body.
- Charcoal outlines, eyes, arms, and legs.
- No body patterns, text, or logos.

### Clothing, accessories, and identity props

- One small muted-blue rectangular satchel worn cross-body.
- The strap crosses diagonally from the upper left to the lower right from the viewer's perspective.

## Flexible attributes

- Arm angle, leg spacing, and eye tilt may change subtly with action.
- The satchel may rest beside the body during sleep or maintenance scenes if its identity remains clear.
- Small scene-specific handheld objects are allowed within the visual budget.

## Contextual variants

- None approved.

## Forbidden drift

- Do not add a separate head, mouth, ears, horns, hair, wings, or tail.
- Do not turn the folded corner into an ear.
- Do not add writing, logos, ruled lines, or printed patterns to the body.
- Do not replace the muted-blue satchel with a book or backpack.
- Do not render as realistic crumpled paper or glossy 3D plastic.

## Expression grammar

| Emotion | Eyes and face | Posture and spacing | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| calm | level oval eyes | balanced stance with open surrounding space | adding a smile |
| tired | slightly lowered eyes | supported or softened posture | tears or collapsing |
| hopeful | subtly raised gaze | slight forward lean toward open space | sparkling anime eyes |
| uncertain | one small eye-angle difference | paused arm and uneven object distance | punctuation symbols |

## Diary behavior

- Preferred scale: small or medium in frame.
- Preferred actions: standing, carrying one small object, pausing near a threshold, quietly observing.
- Suitable symbols: one loose paper edge, a mild breeze cue, or an open path.
- Density constraints: sparse; do not surround the paper body with labels or stationery decoration.

## Unknowns

- Back and side design.
- Satchel interior and fastening details.
- Whether the body can bend or fold beyond the canonical corner.
