# Onboarding Review Levels

Keep one governance standard while adapting how much schema detail the user
must review. Every level preserves the complete evidence ledger and candidate
card in the delivered artifact.

## Quick review

Use when the references are clear enough to propose a safe identity summary,
internally consistent, and authorized. Unknown views or undecided accessories
do not block quick review when they can remain explicitly undecided.

Keep the primary response camera-simple. Show, in this order:

1. one compact approval summary:
   - **Must remain** — proposed immutable identity anchors;
   - **May vary** — flexible or contextual features;
   - **Left undecided** — unknown, inferred, or nonessential details;
   - **Style policy** — the proposed policy and whether per-entry override is allowed;
   - **Storage outcome** — where the candidate is available and whether it is
     transient, export-ready, host-saved, or pack-installed;
2. one approve-or-revise question;
3. a review-artifact receipt identifying where the complete evidence and
   candidate can be inspected.

Do not place the full evidence ledger before the approval question. Deliver
the complete review artifact in the same turn using the first host-supported
method:

1. a separate response artifact;
2. an accessible collapsed section whose summary remains outside the fold;
3. an inline appendix after the approval question.

Do not write to a filesystem merely to simulate a response artifact unless an
exact output target and that write were authorized. A download, attachment, or
runtime artifact remains `export-ready` until persistent saving is separately
verified.

Keep the artifact complete but terse. Avoid repeating the same observation in
prose when it already appears in the evidence ledger and candidate card. Use
short ledger statements, only material anchors and unknowns, and no speculative
expression or diary rules beyond what the evidence safely supports.

For quick review, apply this content budget:

- at most four **Must remain**, three **May vary**, and four **Left
  undecided** summary bullets;
- five to ten material evidence-ledger entries;
- outside the candidate card, summarize Confirmed, Inferred, and Unknown or
  point to exact ledger entries instead of restating every observation;
- at most six items in each candidate-card list;
- two or three expression-grammar rows;
- one concise line for each diary-behavior field.

If the character needs more detail than this budget can safely hold, use
guided review rather than silently omitting material evidence.

Summary approval applies to the exact candidate delivered in the identified
artifact. Do not hide the artifact, change it after approval, or treat content
approval as write approval.

Do not escalate solely because the proposed name or candidate anchors still
await approval; obtaining that approval is the purpose of quick review.
Leave nonessential canonical roles undecided instead of asking the user to
classify every visible item. Quick review ends with one approve-or-revise
question covering the displayed summary.

## Guided review

Use when one or more risk triggers apply:

- reference quality is C or insufficient;
- obstruction or missing views prevent a safe candidate rather than merely
  creating unknowns that can remain undecided;
- references conflict;
- the intended use requires deciding whether an accessory, outfit, prop,
  marking, or style is permanent;
- reference authorization or intended use is unclear;
- the candidate would change an existing canonical rule;
- an exact write target already exists or cannot be compared.

Ask only questions whose answers change identity, style policy, authority, or
the approved write outcome and are required for the current candidate or
requested operation. Group related decisions rather than exposing one schema
field at a time.

## Advanced review

Use when the user requests field-level control, the character has complex
variants, or an audit requires detailed canonical decisions.

Expose the complete evidence ledger, confidence, canonical roles, style
provenance, conflicts, and proposed patch. Advanced review does not weaken or
strengthen the approval gate; it changes only presentation depth.

## Safe defaults

Apply these defaults without asking:

- keep unsupported details `unknown` or `undecided`;
- do not promote clothing, props, or scene treatment into immutable identity;
- use `diary-default` style unless the user explicitly chooses another policy;
- allow per-entry style override;
- use `manual-export` when no persistent binding is verified;
- do not ask for a future storage path when installation is not requested.

Never default:

- reference authorization;
- an ambiguous feature into immutable identity;
- a conflict winner;
- a long-term `reference-guided` or `user-selected` style;
- candidate approval, canonical revision, write approval, replacement, or deletion.
